How to test it

Tab to a chip and press Enter or Space — it is a real disclosure control, with expanded state exposed to assistive technology, and no JavaScript involved. Disable JavaScript entirely and every stamp still opens. Set prefers-reduced-motion: reduce and the 120ms expand disappears. Zoom to 400% and the page body does not scroll sideways; only the table does, inside its own container.
